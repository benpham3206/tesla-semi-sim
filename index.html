Tesla Semi Sim

Couple the trailer, take it up the 4% climb, and back it into the green dock bay. Manage speed, battery and cargo.

Touch: steer with the left pad. Brake and GO are pressure pedals, so press higher for more. D/R changes gear when you're stopped. CAM cycles Chase, Cab and Top; reverse always uses the top-down docking view.
Keyboard: arrows or WASD, R for gear, C for camera, M for sound.
On a phone, use Share → Add to Home Screen to play fullscreen.
